A collection of small natural language processing projects, from tokenisation
to a local chatbot.

<!--more-->

What it includes:

- a local chatbot based on the FLAN-T5 model;
- a study of tokenisation and text data preparation;
- experiments with pretrained models from the Transformers library.

The collection serves as a testing ground for individual techniques before they
find their way into a larger project.
